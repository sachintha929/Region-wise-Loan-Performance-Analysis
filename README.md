# 📊 Region-wise Loan Performance Dashboard

### Excel-Based Business Intelligence & Loan Portfolio Analysis

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents a **Region-wise Loan Performance Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Excel** to analyze the loan portfolio performance of the **Eastern and Uva Regions**.

The dashboard provides an interactive view of key loan performance indicators, including:

* OC loan payment performance
* OC loans not paid during the last three months
* Loan amount analysis
* Active loan distribution
* Zero Bucket loans
* Gross Arrears
* Region-wise performance comparison

A **PowerPoint presentation** was also created to communicate the major findings and business insights from the dashboard.

> **Note:** The analysis is based on aggregated loan-level information. No personally identifiable customer information should be included in the public repository.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

1. Analyze the payment performance of OC loans over the last three months.
2. Compare OC loan performance between the Eastern and Uva Regions.
3. Analyze loans based on amounts **below and above LKR 5,000**.
4. Understand the distribution of Active Loans across regions.
5. Analyze the regional distribution of Zero Bucket loans.
6. Identify the regional contribution to total Gross Arrears.
7. Create an interactive Excel dashboard using slicers.
8. Present actionable business insights through a PowerPoint presentation.

# 📈 Key Analysis & Findings

## 1. OC Payment Performance

Out of the company's OC loans:

* **46%** were paid.
* **54%** were not paid over the last three months.

### Key Finding

> **54% of OC loans were not paid over the last three months.**

This indicates that OC payment performance requires attention at the overall portfolio level.

---

## 2. Region-wise Not Paid OC Loans

| Region         | All OC Loans | Not Paid OC Loans | Paid OC Loans | Not Paid % |
| -------------- | -----------: | ----------------: | ------------: | ---------: |
| Eastern Region |          205 |                89 |           116 |    **43%** |
| Uva Region     |          315 |               194 |           121 |    **62%** |

The Not Paid percentage is calculated as:

**Not Paid OC Loans ÷ All OC Loans × 100**

### Key Finding

* Eastern Region → **43% Not Paid**
* Uva Region → **62% Not Paid**

> **Uva Region has a higher OC Not Paid rate compared with Eastern Region.**

---

## 3. OC Loans — Less Than LKR 5,000

| Region         | All OC Loans | Loans < LKR 5,000 |
| -------------- | -----------: | ----------------: |
| Eastern Region |          205 |                40 |
| Uva Region     |          315 |                73 |

The regional proportions are approximately:

* Eastern Region → **19.5%**
* Uva Region → **23.2%**

### Key Finding

> **Uva Region has a higher proportion of OC loans below LKR 5,000.**

---

## 4. OC Loans — Greater Than LKR 5,000

| Region         | All OC Loans | Loans > LKR 5,000 |
| -------------- | -----------: | ----------------: |
| Eastern Region |          205 |               165 |
| Uva Region     |          315 |               242 |

The regional proportions are approximately:

* Eastern Region → **80.5%**
* Uva Region → **76.8%**

### Key Finding

> **More than three-quarters of OC loans in both regions are above LKR 5,000.**

---

## 5. Active Loans by Amount

### Active Loans — Less Than LKR 5,000

| Region         | All Active Loans | Loans < LKR 5,000 |
| -------------- | ---------------: | ----------------: |
| Eastern Region |              591 |               565 |
| Uva Region     |              425 |               403 |

### Active Loans — Greater Than LKR 5,000

| Region         | All Active Loans | Loans > LKR 5,000 |
| -------------- | ---------------: | ----------------: |

### Key Finding

More than **94% of Active Loans** in both regions are below LKR 5,000.

* Eastern Region → **95.6%**
* Uva Region → **94.8%**

> **The Active Loan portfolio is heavily concentrated in loans below LKR 5,000.**

---

## 6. Total Active Loans by Region

The company has a total of:

# **1,016 Active Loans**

| Region         | Total Active Loans |    Share |
| -------------- | -----------------: | -------: |
| Eastern Region |                591 |  **58%** |
| Uva Region     |                425 |  **42%** |
| **Total**      |          **1,016** | **100%** |

### Key Finding

> **Eastern Region accounts for 58% of the company's Active Loan portfolio.**

Eastern has **166 more Active Loans** than Uva.

---

## 7. Zero Bucket Analysis

The combined Active and OC loan portfolio contains:

# **1,536 Loans**

Out of these:

# **819 Loans are in Zero Bucket**

Regional distribution:

| Region         | Total Loans | Zero Bucket Loans | Share of Total Zero Bucket |
| -------------- | ----------: | ----------------: | -------------------------: |
| Eastern Region |         796 |               507 |                    **62%** |
| Uva Region     |         740 |               312 |                    **38%** |
| **Total**      |   **1,536** |           **819** |                   **100%** |

### Important Definition

In this analysis, a **Zero Bucket loan represents a loan with no arrears/current status**.

### Key Finding

> **Eastern Region contributes 62% of the company's total Zero Bucket loans.**

---

## 8. Zero Bucket — Regional Comparison

To understand the Zero Bucket position within each region, the Zero Bucket loans were compared with each region's total loans.

| Region         | Total Loans | Zero Bucket Loans | Zero Bucket Rate |
| -------------- | ----------: | ----------------: | ---------------: |
| Eastern Region |         796 |               507 |          **64%** |
| Uva Region     |         740 |               312 |          **42%** |

Calculation:

**Zero Bucket Loans ÷ Regional Total Loans × 100**

### Key Finding

* Eastern Region → **64%**
* Uva Region → **42%**

> **Eastern Region has a higher Zero Bucket rate, indicating that a larger proportion of its regional loan portfolio is currently without arrears.**

Eastern has **195 more Zero Bucket loans** than Uva.

---

## 9. Gross Arrears by Region

The total Gross Arrears across both regions is:

# **LKR 7,043,656**

Regional distribution:

| Region         |     Loans |     Gross Arrears | Share of Total Gross Arrears |
| -------------- | --------: | ----------------: | ---------------------------: |
| Eastern Region |       796 |     LKR 3,113,414 |                      **44%** |
| Uva Region     |       740 |     LKR 3,930,242 |                      **56%** |
| **Total**      | **1,536** | **LKR 7,043,656** |                     **100%** |

The percentage is calculated as:

**Regional Gross Arrears ÷ Total Gross Arrears × 100**

### Key Finding

> **Uva Region contributes 56% of the total Gross Arrears despite having fewer loans than Eastern Region.**

Uva has approximately:

**LKR 816,828 more Gross Arrears than Eastern Region.**

# 💡 Business Insights

The analysis highlights different areas of focus for each region.

### Uva Region

Uva shows:

* Higher OC Not Paid percentage
* Higher Gross Arrears contribution
* More OC loans above LKR 5,000 in absolute numbers

Therefore:

> **Uva requires greater attention in terms of OC payment performance and Gross Arrears management.**

### Eastern Region

Eastern shows:

* Larger Active Loan portfolio
* Higher Zero Bucket contribution
* Higher Zero Bucket rate within its regional portfolio

In the context of this analysis, the higher Zero Bucket level indicates a larger share of loans currently without arrears.
